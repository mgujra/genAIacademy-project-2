# Account Management (POL-ACC-04)

## Creating an account

Sign up with an email address or via Google/Apple SSO at lumenleaf.example/signup. Guest checkout is available, but warranty registration and the LumenLeaf app require an account.

## Password reset

Click "Forgot password" on the login page. Reset links expire after 30 minutes. If you signed up with Google or Apple SSO, there is no password to reset — use the SSO provider's sign-in. After 5 failed login attempts the account locks for 15 minutes (error ACC-101 "Account temporarily locked").

## Changing email address

Go to Account → Settings → Email, enter the new address, and confirm via the verification link sent to the new address. The change does not take effect until verified. Order history transfers automatically.

## Deleting your account

Email privacy@lumenleaf.example or use Account → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and completes within 30 days per our privacy policy. Active warranties remain valid after deletion if you keep your proof of purchase, but you lose in-app warranty lookup.

## Two-factor authentication

2FA via authenticator app (TOTP) is available under Account → Settings → Security. SMS 2FA is not supported. If you lose access to your authenticator, use one of the 10 one-time backup codes generated at setup; without backup codes, identity verification through support takes 3–5 business days.

## Newsletter subscription

Subscribe at the site footer for a one-time 10% welcome discount (single use, orders over $30). Unsubscribe anytime via the link in any newsletter email; processing takes up to 48 hours.
