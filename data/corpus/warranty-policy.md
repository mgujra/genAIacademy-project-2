# Warranty Policy (POL-WAR-07)

## Coverage

All LumenLeaf devices carry a 2-year limited warranty from the delivery date, covering manufacturing defects: dead pixels/LED segments, failure to power on, Wi-Fi radio failure, flicker on standard switches, and overload protection failures within rated load. Registering the device in the app within 60 days of purchase extends coverage by 6 months (30 months total).

## What is not covered

Physical damage after delivery, water damage (no LumenLeaf device is rated for outdoor or bathroom use), use with incompatible wall dimmers, electrical surges (use a surge protector for the hub), commercial use beyond 12 hours/day duty cycle, and units with removed serial numbers.

## Making a warranty claim

1. In the app: Device → Settings → Report Problem, or email support@lumenleaf.example with the serial number (printed on the device base) and proof of purchase.
2. Support runs remote diagnostics within 1 business day.
3. Approved claims receive a prepaid return label and a replacement ships within 2 business days of our receiving the defective unit. We replace with the same model or, if discontinued, the nearest current equivalent.

Warranty replacements do not restart the warranty period; the remaining original term applies. Refunds instead of replacement are available only within the 30-day return window (POL-RET-02).

## Out-of-warranty service

We do not offer paid repairs. Out-of-warranty customers receive a 20% discount code toward a replacement purchase of the same product category.

## Battery note

LumenLeaf devices are mains-powered and contain no user-serviceable batteries. The LL-H300 hub's internal RTC battery is rated for the life of the product and is not user-replaceable.
