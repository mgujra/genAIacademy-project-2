# Payments & Billing (POL-PAY-03)

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay. We do not accept checks, money orders, or cryptocurrency. Klarna installment payments are available on orders between $50 and $1,000 in the US only.

## When you are charged

Your payment method is authorized at checkout and captured when the order ships. Pre-orders are charged only at shipment. If a charge appears twice, one is usually a temporary authorization hold that your bank releases within 3–5 business days.

## Common payment errors

- Error PAY-201 "Card declined": the bank refused the authorization. Verify the billing ZIP code matches your statement.
- Error PAY-204 "AVS mismatch": billing address does not match bank records.
- Error PAY-209 "Payment gateway timeout": retry after a few minutes; do not retry more than 3 times or your bank may flag fraud.

## Invoices and receipts

A receipt is emailed at purchase. Business customers can download a VAT/tax invoice from Account → Orders → Download Invoice. Invoices include our tax ID and can be reissued with your company name if you update Billing Profile before requesting.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address. Orders to Oregon, Montana, New Hampshire, and Delaware are not taxed. UK orders include 20% VAT in the displayed price.

## Promo codes

One promo code per order. Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively after an order is placed, and cannot be combined with the 10% newsletter welcome discount.
