# Product Manual: LumenLeaf Hub (LL-H300) & Smart Plug (LL-P50)

## LL-H300 Hub

The hub bridges Thread, Zigbee, and Wi-Fi devices and enables HomeKit for LL-B200 bulbs. It connects to your router via Ethernet (recommended) or 2.4/5 GHz Wi-Fi.

### Setup
1. Connect the hub to power and to your router with the included Ethernet cable.
2. Wait for the status LED to turn solid blue (about 90 seconds on first boot while firmware updates).
3. In the app: Add Device → Hub → scan the QR code on the bottom of the unit.

### LED status codes
- Solid blue: online, normal operation
- Pulsing blue: pairing mode
- Solid amber: firmware updating — do not unplug
- Blinking red: no internet connection (error LL-E30)
- Alternating red/blue: hub factory reset in progress

### Factory reset
Hold the recessed button on the back for 12 seconds until the LED alternates red/blue. This removes all paired devices.

## LL-P50 Smart Plug

15A smart plug with energy monitoring. Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz only. Maximum load 1800W; exceeding the load trips the internal breaker and the plug shows error LL-E45 "Overload protection triggered" in the app. Reset by unplugging the connected appliance and pressing the side button once.

The LL-P50 supports schedules, away-mode randomization, and child lock (disables the physical button). Energy history is stored for 12 months in the app and can be exported as CSV.

## Warranty registration

Register devices under App → Device → Settings → Register Warranty within 60 days of purchase to add 6 bonus months to the standard warranty (see POL-WAR-07).
