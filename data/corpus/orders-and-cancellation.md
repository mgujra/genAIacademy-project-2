# Orders & Cancellation (POL-ORD-05)

## Order statuses

Orders progress through: Placed → Preparing shipment → Shipped → Out for delivery → Delivered. You can see the current status under Account → Orders.

## Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled free of charge while in "Placed" status — typically within 2 hours of purchase. Use Account → Orders → Cancel Order. Once the status is "Preparing shipment" or later, cancellation is no longer possible; instead, refuse delivery or return the item under the 30-day return policy (POL-RET-02). There is no cancellation fee at any stage.

## Changing an order

Items cannot be added to an existing order. To change quantity or swap items while the order is still in "Placed" status, cancel and reorder. Shipping address changes follow the shipping policy (POL-SHIP-01).

## Pre-orders

Pre-order items show an estimated ship date on the product page. You are charged when the item ships, not at checkout. Pre-orders can be cancelled any time before shipment. If an order mixes in-stock and pre-order items, the in-stock items ship immediately and the rest follow at no extra shipping cost.

## Order not received but marked delivered

First check with household members and neighbors, and look for a carrier photo in the tracking link. If the package is still missing 48 hours after the delivery scan, report it via Account → Orders → Report Issue. We replace or refund per the lost-package terms in POL-SHIP-01.

## Bulk and wholesale orders

Orders of 10+ units of one SKU route through our wholesale desk (wholesale@lumenleaf.example) under separate terms (POL-WHL-09), including a 15% restocking fee on returns and net-30 invoicing for approved businesses.
