# Returns & Refunds Policy (POL-RET-02)

## Return window

You may return any LumenLeaf product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, and within 60 days for store credit. Products must be in original packaging with all accessories. Opened products are accepted as long as they are undamaged. Final-sale items (marked on the product page) and gift cards are not returnable.

## How to start a return

Go to Account → Orders → Return Item, select a reason, and print the prepaid return label. Returns are free for defective items. For non-defective returns, a $6.50 label fee is deducted from the refund. Drop the package at any UPS location. You can also start a return by emailing support@lumenleaf.example with your order number.

## Refund processing time

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after our warehouse receives and inspects the return. Store credit is issued within 24 hours of inspection. If a refund has not arrived after 10 business days, ask your bank about pending ACH reversals before contacting us; card issuers occasionally hold refunds for one statement cycle.

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order. If the item is defective, we ship a replacement free of charge without requiring a new order.

## Holiday extension

Purchases made between November 1 and December 24 may be returned until January 31 of the following year.

## Restocking fees

There are no restocking fees for individual customers. Bulk orders (10+ units of the same SKU) carry a 15% restocking fee, per our wholesale terms (POL-WHL-09).
