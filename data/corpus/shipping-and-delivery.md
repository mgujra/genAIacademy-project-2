# Shipping & Delivery Policy (POL-SHIP-01)

LumenLeaf ships to the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom from our fulfillment centers in Reno, NV and Toronto, ON.

## Shipping options and timelines

Standard shipping is free on orders over $49 and costs $5.99 otherwise. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within the continental US, 5–7 business days to Canada, and 7–10 business days to the UK. Expedited shipping ($14.99) delivers in 2 business days within the US only. Orders placed before 1:00 PM Pacific Time ship the same day; orders after the cutoff ship the next business day. We do not ship on weekends or US federal holidays.

## Order tracking

A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of shipment. You can also find it under Account → Orders → Track Shipment. If your tracking page shows "Label created" for more than 3 business days, contact support — the package may be lost in induction.

## Delayed or lost packages

A package is considered delayed after 7 business days past the estimated delivery date, and lost after 14. For lost packages we ship a free replacement or issue a full refund, your choice. We require a police report only for orders above $500.

## Address changes after ordering

We can change a shipping address only before the order status changes to "Preparing shipment" (usually within 2 hours of purchase). After that, we cannot redirect packages. If the package returns to us as undeliverable, we refund the order minus original shipping cost.

## PO boxes and APO/FPO

We deliver to PO boxes via standard shipping only. APO/FPO military addresses are supported with delivery windows of 2–4 weeks.
