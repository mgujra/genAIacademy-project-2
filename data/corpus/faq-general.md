# Frequently Asked Questions (KB-FAQ-08)

**Q: Do LumenLeaf devices work without internet?**
A: Basic on/off via Bluetooth works for the LL-B200 within range. Schedules stored on the LL-H300 hub keep running offline. Cloud features (voice assistants, remote access, energy history sync) require internet.

**Q: Is there a monthly subscription?**
A: No. All app features are free. There are no paid tiers.

**Q: Can I use the bulbs outdoors?**
A: No. All current LumenLeaf products are indoor-only (IP20). Outdoor use voids the warranty.

**Q: How many devices can one account control?**
A: Up to 150 devices per account, and up to 50 devices per hub.

**Q: Can I share access with family?**
A: Yes — Account → Home Sharing invites up to 5 members. Members can control devices but cannot delete the home or remove devices unless granted admin.

**Q: Do you have a trade-in or recycling program?**
A: We don't offer trade-ins. For recycling, LumenLeaf devices are e-waste; many municipal programs accept them. US customers can mail devices back to us for free recycling using the form at lumenleaf.example/recycle.

**Q: Do you offer a student or military discount?**
A: We offer 15% off for verified military and first responders via ID.me at checkout (US only). There is no student discount.

**Q: Where are LumenLeaf products sold?**
A: Direct at lumenleaf.example, plus Amazon US/CA/UK. Devices bought from authorized retailers carry the same warranty; provide the retailer receipt as proof of purchase.

**Q: Is my data sold to third parties?**
A: No. Usage analytics are anonymized; see the privacy policy. You can request data export or deletion at privacy@lumenleaf.example.

**Q: What languages does the app support?**
A: English, Spanish, French, and German.
