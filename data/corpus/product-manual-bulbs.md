# Product Manual: LumenLeaf Smart Bulbs (LL-B100 / LL-B200)

## Models

- LL-B100 Glow: A19, 800 lumens, soft white 2700K, dimmable, Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz.
- LL-B200 Spectrum: A19, 1100 lumens, full color RGBWW 1800K–6500K, dimmable, Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz + Bluetooth pairing.

Both bulbs require a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. 5 GHz-only networks are not supported. No hub is required, but the LL-H300 hub enables Thread and faster scene response.

## Setup

1. Install the bulb and power it on.
2. Open the LumenLeaf app → Add Device → Smart Bulb.
3. The bulb should pulse twice to indicate pairing mode. If it does not pulse, power-cycle it 3 times (on-off, 1 second apart) to factory reset; the bulb will flash 5 times when reset.
4. Follow in-app steps to join your Wi-Fi.

## Voice assistants

Works with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit (LL-B200 only; HomeKit requires the LL-H300 hub or Bluetooth proximity setup).

## Specifications

- Lifespan: 25,000 hours
- Power: LL-B100 9W, LL-B200 11W (equivalent to 60W/75W incandescent)
- Operating temperature: -10°C to 40°C; indoor use only, not rated for enclosed fixtures
- Dimming: app and voice dimming only; wall dimmer switches are NOT supported and cause flicker (see error LL-E12)

## Firmware updates

Firmware updates install automatically overnight when the bulb is idle and connected. Current firmware versions: LL-B100 v3.4.1, LL-B200 v4.2.0. You can check under App → Device → Settings → Firmware.

## Energy monitoring

LL-B200 reports real-time wattage in the app. LL-B100 does not have energy monitoring hardware.
